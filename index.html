Features & Power-Ups

  • HP Cross (+): Heals you for HP up to max. HP overheal.
  • Bonus Time (🕒): Instantly adds seconds to the timer.
  • Coffee Overclock (☕): +% Movement Speed & FOV rush for seconds.
  • Infinite Ammo (⚡): Infinite shotgun ammo without reloading for seconds.
  • Task-Killer (🧯): Freezes all pigs on the map for seconds.
